Our products

Boiler pressure parts and engineered components

Made to drawing by Tenaro Global EPC and Dehu Group companies, and supplied through Stochos for power, process and mining operations across Ghana and West Africa.

Boiler Pressure Parts

Pressure parts for new boilers, replacements and upgrades, made to the boiler maker's or customer's drawings.

Water Wall Panels

  • Front, rear, side and roof panels, supplied with headers
  • Membrane construction with welded IS 2062 mild-steel fins
  • Tubes to BS 3059 ERW
  • For cogeneration boilers from 15 to 50 TPH
Application:
  • Sugar cogeneration boilers
  • Textile process boilers
  • Captive power plants

Superheater Coils

  • Primary, secondary, radiant and convection superheaters
  • U-bends, loose tube bends and support assemblies
  • SA 213 T11 / T22 tubes with SS 304 and SS 310 supports
  • Built to OEM or customer drawings
Application:
  • Power generation
  • Steel and metallurgical plants
  • Boiler OEMs and EPCs

Economisers and Evaporators

  • Bare, finned and spiral-fin economiser coil assemblies
  • Evaporator coil sets and boiler bank tubes
  • BS 3059 Part 1 Gr 320 ERW tubes, OD 63.5 mm and 76.2 mm
  • Finned integral economiser assemblies
Application:
  • Sugar and distilleries
  • Cement waste heat recovery
  • Tyre and rubber plants

Bed and In-Bed Coils

  • Bed and in-bed coils for fluidised bed boilers, made to drawing
  • SA 210 Gr A1 and BS 3059 tubes
  • SS 304 and TP347 studs, ceramic ferrules and plate sleeves
  • IBR approved
Application:
  • Pulp and paper
  • Chemicals and agro-chemicals
  • Food and dairy processing
  • Tyre manufacturing

Steam Headers and Reboilers

  • HP, MP and LP steam headers
  • SA 335 P11, SA 106 Gr B and SA 515 Gr 60
  • Reboilers in SA 213 TP316L
  • Custom studs and attemperator nozzles
Application:
  • Pulp and paper
  • Steel plant heat recovery
  • Boiler OEMs and EPCs

Steam Piping and PRDS Stations

Application:

High-Temperature Alloy Components

  • Fuel nozzle cap plates (coal caps) in SS 310
  • Bed coil sleeves and shield plates in SS 310
  • Fluidising air nozzles and distributor plates
  • Fabrication in SS 304, SS 310 and SS 316
Application:
  • Steel plants
  • Tyre manufacturing
  • Chemical plants
  • Cement plants

Conveyors, Ducting and Structures

  • Screw conveyors for salt, ash and ore
  • Boiler dust ducts, dust breakers and ducting modifications
  • Soot blower platforms, gratings, handrails and columns
  • Support steelwork to drawing
Application:
  • Pulp and paper

  • Mining and minerals
